The real problem

Most agents work on one part. Usually the ads.

Then the ads don't work, and they blame the ads. Here's where it actually breaks.

01

"I sell Medicare" is not a market.

Talk to everyone and nobody stops scrolling. Turning 65, delayed Part B and supplement-only are three different people with three different problems.

02

The ad has no job.

No hook, no problem, no promise, no clear next step. It looks like every other agent's ad, so it gets treated like one.

03

The lead goes cold after the click.

Called the next day. No reminders. No follow-up. The same 100 leads that make one agent two clients make another agent eight.

You don't have a lead problem.

You have a machine problem.
